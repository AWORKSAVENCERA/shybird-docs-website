Responsible AI

Purpose

This document describes our approach to the responsible design, development, deployment and operation of artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities within our Shybird product suite.

Our objective is to ensure that AI technologies are used in a manner that is ethical, transparent, secure, accountable and aligned with customer interests.

This document applies to all AI-enabled features provided as part of our services.

Our commitment

We are committed to the responsible use of AI and to ensuring that AI capabilities support, rather than undermine, human decision-making.

Our approach is guided by the following principles:

  • Human oversight and accountability
  • Transparency and explainability
  • Privacy and data protection
  • Security and resilience
  • Fairness and non-discrimination
  • Reliability and quality
  • Legal and regulatory compliance
  • Continuous monitoring and improvement

Scope of AI usage

AI capabilities within the platform may be used to support functions such as:

  • Content generation
  • Content summarisation
  • Search and information retrieval
  • Recommendations and guidance
  • Classification and categorisation
  • Workflow automation
  • Natural language interactions
  • Analytical insights

The availability and functionality of AI features may vary by product, tenant or subscription level.

Human oversight

AI outputs are intended to support users and not to replace professional judgement, expertise or organisational decision-making.

Our approach includes:

  • Maintaining human accountability for business decisions
  • Providing users with the ability to review AI-generated outputs
  • Encouraging validation of important recommendations and conclusions
  • Applying additional review processes where higher-risk use cases are identified

Users remain responsible for evaluating the suitability of AI-generated content for their specific circumstances.

Transparency

We aim to be transparent about where AI technologies are used within our services.

Where appropriate, users may be informed when:

  • Content has been generated by AI
  • Recommendations have been produced by AI models
  • AI-assisted functionality is being used
  • Automated processing forms part of a workflow

We seek to provide sufficient information to enable informed use of AI-assisted features.

Accuracy and reliability

AI systems are probabilistic in nature and may occasionally produce inaccurate, incomplete or misleading outputs.

To mitigate these risks we may:

  • Evaluate AI capabilities before deployment
  • Conduct testing against representative use cases
  • Monitor performance and quality
  • Implement guardrails and validation controls
  • Investigate reported issues
  • Continuously improve AI-enabled services

Despite these measures, AI-generated outputs should be reviewed before being relied upon for important decisions.

Fairness and bias management

We recognise that AI systems may produce biased or unintended outcomes.

To manage these risks we seek to:

  • Evaluate AI capabilities before implementation
  • Assess potential impacts on users
  • Monitor for unintended outcomes
  • Review user feedback and reported concerns
  • Apply corrective actions where required

We do not intentionally design AI systems to unlawfully discriminate against individuals or groups.

Privacy and data protection

The use of AI capabilities is subject to our data protection and privacy controls.

We are committed to:

  • Processing personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently
  • Applying appropriate security measures
  • Supporting data minimisation principles
  • Restricting access to authorised personnel
  • Managing retention and disposal requirements
  • Maintaining appropriate processor and supplier agreements

Where AI capabilities process personal data, such processing is governed by our privacy and data protection policies.

Customer data and AI training

Unless explicitly agreed with a customer or stated within contractual documentation, customer data is not used to train proprietary or third-party foundation models.

Where AI service providers are utilised, contractual and technical controls are implemented to support the protection of customer information.

Customers are encouraged to review service-specific documentation relating to AI-enabled features and associated data processing activities.

Security of AI services

AI services are incorporated into our wider information security framework.

Security measures may include:

  • Access controls
  • Authentication mechanisms
  • Secure system design
  • Logging and monitoring
  • Incident management procedures
  • Supplier assurance activities
  • Vulnerability management processes

Security risks associated with AI capabilities are considered as part of ongoing risk management activities.

Environmental sustainability and AI

We recognise that the development, training and operation of AI systems can consume significant computational resources and energy.

When designing, deploying and operating AI-enabled features, we seek to consider their environmental impact alongside customer value, security, privacy and performance requirements.

Our approach may include:

  • Evaluating whether AI is necessary to achieve a particular outcome
  • Selecting appropriately sized models for specific use cases
  • Avoiding unnecessary processing and excessive computation
  • Optimising prompts, workflows and system architecture to improve efficiency
  • Monitoring the utilisation of AI services and infrastructure
  • Removing or redesigning AI features that do not provide sufficient user value
  • Considering the sustainability practices of AI and cloud service providers
  • Promoting efficient use of computing resources throughout the product lifecycle

Proportionate use of AI

We aim to use AI only where it provides a clear benefit to customers, users or business processes.

Where a non-AI solution can achieve the same outcome with lower complexity, lower resource consumption or reduced operational risk, this option may be preferred.

This approach supports responsible innovation while helping to minimise unnecessary environmental impacts.

Sustainable technology choices

When selecting AI technologies, suppliers and deployment architectures, we may consider factors including:

  • Resource efficiency
  • Infrastructure utilisation
  • Scalability
  • Service reliability
  • Environmental commitments of suppliers
  • Estimated operational impact

Environmental considerations form one of several factors used during technology selection and solution design.

Monitoring and continuous improvement

We seek to continually improve the efficiency of AI-enabled services through:

  • Periodic review of AI usage patterns
  • Evaluation of emerging technologies and models
  • Removal of redundant or low-value AI workloads
  • Optimisation of system performance
  • Review of supplier sustainability commitments

As AI technology evolves, we will continue to evaluate opportunities to reduce environmental impact while maintaining service quality, security and customer value.

Risk management

AI-related risks are managed through our established governance processes.

This may include assessment of risks relating to:

  • Privacy
  • Security
  • Bias
  • Accuracy
  • Reliability
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Intellectual property
  • Customer impact

Appropriate mitigations are identified, implemented and reviewed as part of ongoing risk management activities.

Automated decision-making

Our services are not intended to make legally binding or solely automated decisions affecting individuals without appropriate human oversight.

Where automated processing forms part of a workflow, organisations remain responsible for ensuring that its use complies with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Supplier and model governance

Where third-party AI models or AI service providers are used, we seek to perform appropriate due diligence and ongoing oversight.

Assessment activities may include consideration of:

  • Security controls
  • Privacy protections
  • Service reliability
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Contractual commitments
  • Technical capabilities
  • Supplier risk

Supplier relationships are reviewed through our supplier governance processes.

Incident management

Any identified AI-related incidents, vulnerabilities or concerns may be managed through established incident management procedures.

Activities may include:

  • Investigation
  • Risk assessment
  • Containment actions
  • Corrective measures
  • Customer communications where appropriate
  • Continuous improvement activities

Monitoring and continuous improvement

AI capabilities are reviewed periodically to evaluate effectiveness, safety, accuracy and alignment with organisational objectives.

Inputs to improvement activities may include:

  • User feedback
  • Incident reports
  • Regulatory developments
  • Risk assessments
  • Security reviews
  • Supplier updates
  • Product reviews
  • Audit findings

Appropriate actions are prioritised and implemented through normal governance processes.

Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

  • Determining whether AI-generated outputs are appropriate for their purposes
  • Implementing organisational governance for AI usage
  • Providing suitable training and guidance to users
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements
  • Reviewing AI-generated content before acting upon it

AI outputs should not be treated as legal, financial, medical or other professional advice unless independently verified by appropriately qualified professionals.

Future regulatory compliance

We monitor developments in AI-related laws, regulations and standards and seek to adapt our governance framework accordingly.

Where applicable, we will update our policies, processes and controls to support evolving regulatory requirements and recognised best practices.

Additional assurance information

Subject to appropriate confidentiality arrangements, we may provide additional information relating to AI governance, including:

  • AI risk assessment methodologies
  • AI feature descriptions
  • Supplier assurance information
  • Privacy impact assessments
  • Security controls
  • Testing approaches
  • Governance frameworks
