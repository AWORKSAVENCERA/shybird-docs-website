Getting started with Shybird Deliver

Get started with Shybird Deliver, where you’ll find everything you need to study online — your course materials, learning activities, assessments and announcements, all in one place.

Getting started

Shybird Deliver is the online platform for your course(s). It gives you access to everything you need for your studies in one place. Shybird Deliver is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and you can access it from a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. You will gain access to your course once you are fully enrolled.

Sign in

Signing in after enrolment

Users will first have to be enrolled by the organisation’s admin onto a course to receive the URL for sign in. Enrolled users will receive an invitation from no-reply@avencera.ai with a ‘Activate Your Account’ email. Press the ‘Activate Your Account’ button embedded within this email to be taken to the sign up page.

After activating your account, to log in, go to the Shybird Deliver login page using the URL below. Enter the email address that you used for enrolment, and press sign in. You will then be asked to enter a one-time verification code, which will have been sent to your email. Enter this code then enter.

Users can also use the ‘Sign In with Google’ or ‘Sign in with Microsoft’ buttons to be redirected to separate login processes. You can access Shybird Deliver on the go from the web browser on your phone or tablet, so you can keep up with your course wherever you are. The layout adjusts to fit smaller screens.

Users can sign in here: Avencera LMS - Participant Login

If you can’t log in

Check that you are using the correct email address and that your account has been activated – attempting to use the original enrolment email from no-reply@avencera.ai will take you to an ‘Invitation Used’ page if your account has been activated.

Following this, check your internet connection is still working on another separate site.

If you still can’t sign in, consult your organisations admin or helpdesk.

Finding your way around

Multiple functions exist within Deliver, all listed on the left hand side of your screen. Below lists the functions available and the purpose of each.

Courses

When you log in, you’ll land on your ’Course Overview’ — your personal home page that allows you to access the courses you’re enrolled on, or view other courses you may wish to register interest on.

Events

Click on ‘Events’ to view and manage the events you are signed up to. If you are not signed up to any events, ‘No events available’ will appear on your screen.

Resource Library

Click on ‘Resource Library’ to access resources and learning materials across your courses. If your enrolled courses do not have an resources associated with it, ‘No resources available’ will appear on your screen.

Content Questions

Click on ‘Content Questions’ to access your AI enabled learning assistant. Use the provided chat function to ask the Learning Assistant questions about your learnings.

Technical Questions

Click on ‘Technical Questions’ to access FAQs or get in touch with support.

Notifications

Click on ‘Notifications’ to see updates about courses and activities. You can filter for all or unread notifications in the top left corner.

Settings

See the dedicated ‘Settings’ section below.

Dark Mode

Press the slider here to switch between Dark and Light mode.

Logging out

To log out of the system, click the sign out icon to securely log out of your account. You will need to sign in again to access the application.

Settings

The Settings area is opened from ‘Settings’ in the menu on the left.

Profile & security

The ‘Profile and Security’ section lets you manage your personal details, choose how we remind you about your learning, and see the devices you’re signed in on.

Your profile details

Your personal details are shown on the right of the ‘Profile and Security’ panel. You can edit your full name, email address, department, job title, organisation name and sector. Make your changes, then click ‘Save Changes’ to update your profile.

Profile photo

Your profile photo appears on the left of the panel. Click ‘Change Photo’ to upload a new image, or ‘Remove Photo’ to delete your current one.

Learning reminders

Under ‘Learning Reminders’, you can choose how Shybird Deliver reminds you about courses in progress:

  • Reminders – get a nudge when you haven’t visited a course in a while. Use the toggle to turn this on or off.
  • Email reminders – also receive reminders by email, not just in the app. Use the toggle to turn this on or off.
  • Pause reminders – take a break without turning reminders off for good. Choose ‘Pause 2 weeks’, ‘Pause 1 month’ or ‘Pause 3 months’.

Active sessions

Under ‘Active Sessions’, you can see the devices where you’re currently logged in and when each was last active.

To sign out of a device, click ‘End Session’ next to it — this is useful if you’ve signed in on a shared or public computer.

Accessing your course

Finding enrolled courses

Your courses are shown on the ‘Course Overview’ page. To narrow down what’s displayed, select the ‘All Courses’ dropdown in the top right of the screen. This will allow you to filter for courses that have been ‘Assigned’ to you.

Each enrolled course has a ‘Start Course’ button — select it to open the course.

Inside a course

Course content is organised into sections (for example, by week or by module). Each section brings together the learning materials, activities and links you need. You can move between them using the navigation menu.

Onboarding

When you open your course for the first time, you may be asked some onboarding questions. These can vary between courses. You will only need to answer them once..

Learning path

When you select ‘Start Course’, and have completed any onboarding questions, you’ll be taken to the course’s ‘Learning Path’. This page lists every module of your course in the order it should be completed and shows your progress through each one.

The course title and module reference are shown at the top of the page.

A learning path typically includes:

  • Pre-course questionnaire – a short set of questions to complete before you begin.
  • Modules – the main learning content, each made up of a number of lessons. Each module shows its estimated reading and watching time, the number of lessons, and a progress bar showing how many lessons you’ve completed.
  • Tests – short assessments to check your understanding.
  • Post-course questionnaire – a short set of questions to complete once you’ve finished all modules.
  • Download certificate – your certificate of completion for the course.

The modules included can vary between courses. For some courses, modules may be optional.

Moving through your course

Completing modules in order

Modules are unlocked one at a time, so you’ll need to complete each module before moving on to the next (only true if modules are not optional). The button on the right of each module shows its status:

  • Completed – you’ve finished this module.
  • Start Module – this module is available to begin.
  • Complete prerequisite first – this module is locked until you finish the previous module.
  • Complete modules first – this module is locked until you finish all modules.

Your progress is saved automatically, so you can leave and return to a module at any time and continue where you left off.

Types of modules

You can expect to see a variety of content in the modules, including videos, articles, links to other websites, quiz questions, or Likert scales questions.

Move through your modules by completing the required activities and clicking the ‘Continue’ button in the bottom right corner at the end of each section. Click the ‘Exit Module’ button in the top right to return to the Learning Path.

Learning assistant

As you move through content within a learning path, the AI-enabled Learning assistant will be available to you in the bottom right as the multi-coloured Avencera logo. Selecting this will open up the chat with the learning assistant, which can support you with explaining learning content in a personalised manner.

Drag this button to move the Learning Assistant elsewhere if required.

Downloading your certificate

Once you’ve completed all modules in your learning path, the ‘Download Certificate’ button will become available. Select it to download your certificate of completion.

Getting help

Both FAQs and Support can be found using this link: Avencera LMS – Technical Questions

FAQs

If you need help, try reading over the FAQs section:

  • Exit any module using the ‘Exit Module’ button, returning you to the ‘Course Overview’ page.
  • Select the ‘Technical Questions’ button, as indicated by the headphone button.
  • Select ‘Frequently Asked Questions’.

From here, you can view answers to FAQs.

Get support

If you need help using Shybird Deliver or have a question about your course, support is available through reaching our support team:

  • Exit any module using the ‘Exit Module’ button, returning you to the ‘Course Overview’ page.
  • Click the ‘Technical Questions’ button, as indicated by the headphone button.
  • Click ‘Contact Us’.
  • Move through the potential quick fixes, as listed, clicking the checkbox for each to confirm if this has been tried and tested.
  • Once all these have been marked as completed, the ‘Get Support’ button will become available.
  • You can view your requests in the top right, using the ‘My Requests’ button. Fill out the form and press ‘Send Request’ for your support to go to our support team.

Access after your course ends

Your access to Shybird Deliver will remain available for a limited period after your course ends. We recommend downloading and saving any materials, feedback or submitted work you wish to keep before your access is removed. Check any relevant organisational polices to confirm if your organisation allows for this in advance.

Glossary

Word Meaning
Course A complete learning programme that you’re enrolled on, made up of modules that together form your full learning pathway.
Module A single section of a course, taken as one step in that course’s learning pathway.
Certificate Your proof of completing a course. You can download it once you’ve finished every module in your learning path.
Enrolment The process of being added to a course by your organisation’s admin, which gives you access to it.
Learning Assistant An AI-enabled chat tool that answers your questions and explains course content in a way that’s personalised to you.
Learning path The ordered list of every module in your course, showing what to complete and the order to complete it in.
Onboarding A short set of questions you may be asked the first time you open a course. You only need to answer them once.
Resource Library A place to access resources and learning materials from across all of your courses.
