## Commitment to accessibility

We are committed to making our products and services accessible to all users, including people with disabilities.

Our objective is to design, develop and maintain our products in accordance with the principles of inclusive design and the requirements of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

Accessibility is considered throughout our product lifecycle, including design, development, testing, deployment and ongoing improvement activities.

## Accessibility standards

We aim to conform with:

- Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA
- Applicable accessibility legislation and regulations in the jurisdictions in which our services are provided
- Relevant industry best practices for accessible digital services

## Measures we take

To support accessibility, we:

- Incorporate accessibility requirements into product design and development processes
- Apply semantic HTML and accessible user interface patterns
- Support keyboard-only navigation wherever functionality is available
- Ensure sufficient colour contrast and text readability
- Provide appropriate alternative text for meaningful images
- Ensure forms are labelled and accessible to assistive technologies
- Test compatibility with commonly used assistive technologies and modern browsers
- Conduct accessibility reviews during product development and release cycles
- Provide accessibility awareness and training for relevant personnel

## Conformance status

We are working towards full conformance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

Accessibility assessments are performed periodically using a combination of:

- Automated accessibility testing tools
- Manual accessibility reviews
- Keyboard navigation testing
- Assistive technology testing, where appropriate

Any issues identified are prioritised and addressed through our normal product development and continuous improvement processes.

## Known limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some areas of the service may not yet achieve complete WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance.

Examples may include:

- Third-party content or integrations that are outside our direct control
- Legacy functionality awaiting redesign or enhancement
- Newly released features undergoing accessibility verification

Where limitations are identified, we aim to provide reasonable alternatives and remediation plans.

## Requesting accessible alternatives

If you experience difficulty accessing any content, feature or functionality, or if you require information in an alternative format, please contact us.

When contacting us, please provide:

- The page or feature you were using
- A description of the issue encountered
- Any assistive technology being used (if relevant)
- Your preferred format for any requested information

We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or service in an accessible format.

## Continuous improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing commitment rather than a one-time activity.

We regularly review:

- Accessibility testing results
- User feedback
- Changes to accessibility standards
- Product roadmap initiatives
- Emerging assistive technology requirements

Findings from these reviews inform our prioritisation and continuous improvement activities.
