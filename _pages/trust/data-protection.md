## Purpose

This document explains how we protect, manage, retain and dispose of information processed through our Shybird suite of products.

We are committed to handling personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently, and to complying with applicable data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

This statement is intended for customers, users and prospective customers undertaking procurement, due diligence or assurance activities.

## Data protection principles

We process personal data in accordance with the following principles:

- Lawfulness, fairness and transparency
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimisation
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation
- Integrity and confidentiality
- Accountability

These principles are incorporated into our governance, product design and operational processes.

## Data controller and processor roles

The role we perform depends on the nature of the information being processed.

### Customer content

For customer content uploaded to or created within Shybird, including qualification materials, course content and any personal data contained within those materials, the customer organisation is normally the data controller.

Avencera Ltd acts as a data processor and processes such information only on the documented instructions of the customer.

Our suppliers and development parters acts as sub-processors to Avencera in operating the platform.

### Service administration data

For information required to operate, secure, support and administer the service, such as user accounts, billing information, support requests and security logs, Avencera Ltd acts as the data controller.

In these circumstances, Avencera determines the purposes and means of processing and is responsible for meeting the associated data protection obligations.

## What information we process

The platform enables users to upload source materials from which qualifications, courses, learning outcomes and assessment content may be generated.

Examples of information processed may include:

- Course specifications
- Qualification documentation
- Learning materials
- Assessment materials
- User account information
- Support and operational records
- System usage information

### Personal data within uploaded materials

Source materials may occasionally contain personal data, such as:

- Names of authors
- Names of contributors
- Details contained within case studies
- Contact information included within source documentation

We do not require personal data to be included in source materials and encourage customers to avoid including unnecessary personal information wherever possible.

Where personal data is present, we process only the information necessary to provide the service.

## Why we process information

We process information for purposes including:

- Provision of the platform and associated services
- Generation of qualification and learning content
- User authentication and account management
- Customer support
- Security monitoring and incident management
- Service improvement
- Compliance with legal obligations
- Business continuity and disaster recovery activities

We do not use customer content for marketing purposes.

## Legal bases for processing

Depending on the circumstances, we may rely on one or more legal bases for processing personal data, including:

- Performance of a contract
- Legitimate interests
- Compliance with legal obligations
- Consent where required by law

The specific legal basis will depend on the nature of the processing activity.

## Privacy by design and default

Privacy considerations are incorporated into the design and operation of our services.

Our approach includes:

- Data minimisation
- Role-based access controls
- Secure system design
- Encryption and security controls
- Retention and disposal controls
- Audit and accountability measures
- Risk assessment activities

Privacy and security requirements are considered throughout the product lifecycle.

## Artificial intelligence processing

Shybird uses artificial intelligence technologies to assist users in analysing source materials and generating learning and assessment content.

AI-generated outputs are intended to support user activities and are not automatically published or deployed without human review.

Every output is reviewed by a person before it is finalised.

Further information regarding our approach to AI governance is available within our [Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence Statement](/trust/responsible-ai).

## International transfers

Customer content and generated outputs are primarily stored within the United Kingdom.

Some processing activities may involve service providers located outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, we implement appropriate safeguards, which may include:

- International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs)
- UK Addendums to Standard Contractual Clauses
- Adequacy regulations where applicable
- Contractual and organisational safeguards

Further information is available on request.

## Information security

We maintain technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or unauthorised access.

Controls may include:

- Encryption in transit
- Encryption at rest
- Role-based access controls
- Multi-factor authentication
- Security monitoring
- Vulnerability management
- Incident management procedures
- Secure software development practices

Further information is available within our [Information Security Overview](/trust/security).

## Retention of information

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected and to meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

### Customer content

Customer content remains available while the customer maintains an active account and continues to use the service.

### Deleted content

Where content is deleted by a customer, the content enters a controlled holding period for recovery and operational resilience purposes.

Deleted content is normally retained for up to 90 days before permanent disposal.

### Account closure

Where an account is terminated or a customer relationship ends:

- Access to the service is removed
- Customer content enters the retention period
- Data is subsequently disposed of according to documented disposal procedures
- Remaining legal or contractual retention requirements are applied where necessary

### Operational records

Certain operational, audit, security and financial records may be retained for longer periods where required for:

- Legal obligations
- Regulatory compliance
- Security investigations
- Accounting and taxation requirements
- Dispute resolution

## Data disposal and destruction

We maintain procedures to ensure information is securely disposed of when retention periods expire.

Disposal methods may include:

- Permanent deletion
- Cryptographic erasure
- Secure destruction of backups according to retention schedules
- Anonymisation where continued statistical or analytical use is required

Disposal activities are designed to prevent the unauthorised recovery or reconstruction of information.

## Data subject rights

Individuals may have rights under applicable data protection legislation, including the right to:

- Access personal data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of personal data
- Restrict processing
- Object to processing
- Receive personal data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis for processing

Requests are assessed and responded to in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

We aim to respond to valid requests within one calendar month.
