## Purpose

This document provides an overview of the information security controls, governance arrangements and operational practices used to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of our Software as a Service (SaaS) platform and customer information.

The objective of our information security programme is to safeguard customer data, support the reliable delivery of services and maintain compliance with applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

This document is intended for customers, prospective customers and auditors undertaking procurement, assurance or due diligence activities.

## Scope

This document applies to:

- SaaS applications and services
- Production and supporting environments
- Customer data and information assets
- Employees, contractors and authorised users
- Operational processes supporting service delivery
- Third-party suppliers that process or support customer information

## Security governance

Information security is managed through a formal governance framework that supports risk management, compliance and continual improvement.

Our security objectives are to:

- Protect customer information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss
- Support service availability and resilience
- Manage security risks appropriately
- Maintain regulatory and contractual compliance
- Promote security awareness throughout the organisation

Security performance is reviewed regularly through management oversight, risk management and assurance activities.

## Security principles

Our security programme is guided by the following principles:

- Security by design
- Security by default
- Least privilege access
- Defence in depth
- Risk-based decision making
- Continuous improvement
- Accountability and traceability
- Privacy and security integration

Security requirements are considered throughout the product lifecycle.

## Security organisation and responsibilities

Defined responsibilities exist for managing information security across the organisation.

### Executive leadership

Responsible for:

- Security oversight
- Risk appetite
- Strategic direction
- Allocation of resources

### Product and engineering teams

Responsible for:

- Secure design and development
- Security testing
- Vulnerability remediation
- Secure deployment practices

### Operations teams

Responsible for:

- Monitoring and alerting
- Incident response
- Service resilience
- Access administration

### Employees and contractors

Responsible for:

- Compliance with security policies
- Protection of information assets
- Incident reporting
- Security awareness obligations

## Risk management

Security risks are managed through a structured risk management process.

Activities may include:

- Risk identification
- Threat assessment
- Vulnerability assessment
- Control selection
- Risk treatment planning
- Ongoing review and monitoring

Security risks are reviewed periodically and following significant changes, incidents or emerging threats.

## Access control

Access to systems and information is managed using the principle of least privilege.

Controls may include:

- Role-based access control
- User authentication requirements
- Multi-factor authentication
- Segregation of duties
- Privileged access management
- Access approval workflows
- Regular access reviews

Access rights are granted according to business need and reviewed periodically.

## Identity and authentication

Authentication controls are implemented to protect customer and administrative accounts.

These controls may include:

- Strong password requirements
- Multi-factor authentication
- Session management controls
- Account lockout protections
- Secure credential storage
- Monitoring of authentication events

Security-sensitive administrative access is subject to enhanced controls.

## Data protection

Customer information is protected through a combination of organisational and technical controls.

Measures may include:

- Data classification
- Encryption in transit
- Encryption at rest
- Access controls
- Data retention controls
- Secure disposal processes
- Audit logging

Personal data is processed in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

## Secure software development

Security is incorporated into the software development lifecycle.

Practices may include:

- Security requirements definition
- Secure coding standards
- Code review processes
- Dependency management
- Vulnerability scanning
- Security testing
- Change management controls

Security considerations are assessed before significant releases are deployed.

## Vulnerability management

We maintain processes for identifying, assessing and remediating vulnerabilities.

Activities may include:

- Automated scanning
- Dependency analysis
- Threat intelligence monitoring
- Security testing
- Prioritised remediation

Vulnerabilities are assessed according to their severity, likelihood and potential impact.

### Typical remediation targets

| Severity | Target remediation period |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| Critical | As soon as practicable, typically within 72 hours |
| High | Typically within 14 days |
| Medium | Typically within 30 days |
| Low | Risk-based scheduling |

Actual remediation times may vary depending on operational risk and mitigating controls.

## Security testing and assurance

Security assurance activities may include:

- Vulnerability assessments
- Penetration testing
- Configuration reviews
- Secure code review
- Architecture review
- Risk assessments

Significant findings are tracked through corrective action processes until resolved or formally accepted.

## Logging and monitoring

Security monitoring capabilities are maintained to support the detection and investigation of potential security events.

Monitoring activities may include:

- Authentication monitoring
- Privileged access monitoring
- Infrastructure monitoring
- Application monitoring
- Network monitoring
- Security event analysis

Logs are retained in accordance with operational, legal and regulatory requirements.

## Incident management

We maintain an incident management process for the detection, assessment, escalation, investigation and resolution of security incidents.

The process supports:

- Rapid identification of incidents
- Containment and mitigation
- Investigation and analysis
- Recovery activities
- Customer communications where appropriate
- Lessons learned and continual improvement

Security incidents are managed according to defined severity classifications and escalation procedures.

## Business continuity and disaster recovery

Information security is integrated with business continuity and disaster recovery planning.

Measures may include:

- Backup and recovery capabilities
- Service resilience controls
- Incident response coordination
- Disaster recovery testing
- Recovery planning

Further information is available within our Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Overview.

## Supplier security management

Third-party suppliers are assessed according to the risks associated with the services they provide.

Activities may include:

- Supplier due diligence
- Security reviews
- Contractual security requirements
- Performance monitoring
- Periodic reassessment

Critical suppliers may be subject to enhanced oversight arrangements.

## Personnel security

Employees and contractors are subject to appropriate personnel security controls.

Measures may include:

- Recruitment screening
- Confidentiality obligations
- Security awareness training
- Acceptable use requirements
- Security policy acknowledgement

Access to information is removed or adjusted promptly when responsibilities change or engagement ends.

## Security awareness and training

Security awareness forms part of our security programme.

Training activities may include:

- Information security awareness
- Data protection and privacy
- Phishing awareness
- Secure handling of information
- Incident reporting responsibilities

Training is reviewed and refreshed periodically.

## Compliance and assurance

We maintain policies, procedures and controls designed to support compliance with applicable obligations.

Depending on the service and customer requirements, these may include:

- UK GDPR
- Data Protection Act 2018
- Public sector contractual requirements
- Accessibility requirements
- Cyber security obligations
- Industry standards and recognised good practice

Evidence may be made available under appropriate confidentiality arrangements.

## Shared responsibility model

Security is a shared responsibility between the service provider and the customer.

### Our responsibilities

Typically include:

- Operating and securing the SaaS platform
- Protecting infrastructure within our control
- Managing application security
- Security monitoring and incident response
- Backup and recovery activities

### Customer responsibilities

Typically include:

- Managing user accounts and permissions
- Configuring organisational policies
- Protecting endpoint devices
- User awareness and training
- Reviewing audit information
- Managing customer-controlled integrations

## Continuous improvement

Information security is subject to continual review and improvement.

Inputs include:

- Risk assessments
- Security incidents
- Vulnerability findings
- Audit results
- Customer feedback
- Threat intelligence
- Regulatory developments

Improvement actions are prioritised and tracked through established governance processes.
