## Who we are 

Avencera Ltd is the data controller for Shybird. Trao is our technology partner and acts as data processor, building and operating the platform on our instructions. 

## What we collect and why 

When you use Shybird, you upload source documents (such as existing course or qualification materials) so the platform can help generate qualification structures, learning outcomes and assessment content. We process this information to provide that service to you. 

Source documents may incidentally contain personal data, for example names of authors or individuals referenced in case studies. We do not ask for this data specifically, and we keep only what is necessary to provide the service. 

## How AI is used 

Shybird uses AI to help extract and generate content from what you upload. Every output is reviewed by a person before it is finalised, nothing is published automatically. A fuller explanation of how the AI works and what humans control is available in our [Responsible AI statement](/trust/responsible-ai). 

## Who we share data with 

We share data with the following organisations so that Shybird can operate: 

| Organisation | Role | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Trao | Processor | Builds and operates the Shybird platform on our behalf |
| Anthropic | Subprocessor | AI processing (primary production provider) |
| OpenAI | Subprocessor | AI processing (specific functions) |
| Resend | Subprocessor | Sending email notifications |
| DigitalOcean | Subprocessor | Hosting (UK) |
| Qdrant Cloud | Subprocessor | Hosting for search functionality (UK, London) |
| Firecrawl, Tavily, Lightcast, O*NET | Subprocessor | Supporting search functions |

Anthropic is the AI provider used to power Shybird. DeepSeek was evaluated during development. Its use was deferred following an internal review on 29 July 2026, in favour of Anthropic. DeepSeek has not processed and does not process any production data or client information. We are telling you this proactively because we think you should know which AI models have been involved in building the product, not only which one is live today. 

## Where your data is stored 

Your documents and the content generated from them are stored in the UK. Some processing takes place with providers based in the United States, as set out above. Where data leaves the UK, we rely on legal safeguards that are set out in full in our International Transfer Mechanism document, available on request. 

## How long we keep your data 

We keep your data for as long as you are actively using the platform. If you delete something, or your account is closed, it moves to a temporary holding period of 90 days before it is permanently deleted from every system it was stored in. Full detail is available on request. 

## Your rights 

You can ask us to: 

- Access the personal data we hold about you 
- Correct inaccurate data 
- Delete your data (subject to any legal reason we may need to keep it) 
- Restrict or object to how we use it 
- Receive a copy in a portable format 

We will respond within one calendar month. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
