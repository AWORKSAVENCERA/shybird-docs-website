The project dashboard has a sidebar on the left. From there you can manage your notifications and settings, and view recently deleted projects.

## How do I manage notifications?

Select the bell icon in the sidebar to open **Notifications**. A number on the icon shows how many notifications you've received. Notifications tell you when projects have been published and when funding rules have been updated.

- To show all notifications or only unread ones, use the filter at the top left.
- To mark a notification as read, select it. To mark them all as read, select **Mark all as read** in the top right corner.
- To dismiss a notification, select its **X** icon.

## How do I change my profile?

Select the gear icon in the sidebar to open **Settings**. There you can:

- edit your first and last name, then select **Save Changes**. You can't change your email address here.
- change your profile photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB), or remove it.

## How do I change my password?

In **Settings**, change your password in two steps: first verify your current password, then set a new one. Changing your password signs out your other sessions.

If you've forgotten your password, see [What if I've forgotten my password?]({% link _pages/define/sign-in.md %}#what-if-ive-forgotten-my-password)

## How do I find a recently deleted project?

Select the trash icon in the sidebar to open your recently deleted projects. From there you can:

- select **Restore** to restore a deleted project
- select **Delete Permanently** to delete it for good.

Projects stay there for 90 days. See [How do I delete and restore a project?]({% link _pages/define/projects.md %}#how-do-i-delete-and-restore-a-project)
