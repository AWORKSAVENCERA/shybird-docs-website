Shybird Define turns a few strategy documents into a complete, publishable qualification specification. It guides you through every step, from scoping and research to design, validation and publication.

## Before you start

- **You need an invitation.** There is no public sign-up. An administrator invites you by email. See [Sign in to Shybird Define]({% link _pages/define/sign-in.md %}).
- **Use a desktop browser.** Shybird Define is built for a desktop browser on a laptop or desktop computer. On phones and tablets you'll see a 'Please open on desktop' screen. Your browser window also needs to be full size to use it.
- **Have your documents ready.** You'll upload documents that describe the qualification you want to build. At a minimum, we recommend your product strategy and its business case. Your design rules, if you have them, can prescribe KSBs, learning outcomes and units.
