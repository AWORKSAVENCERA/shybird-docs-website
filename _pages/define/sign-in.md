## How do I get an account?

Shybird Define is invite-only, so there is no public sign-up. You join when an administrator invites you by email. The email contains a join link.

1. Open the join link. Shybird Define checks the invitation and shows your name, email address and role, plus any personal message from the person who invited you.
2. Choose a password of at least 8 characters, and confirm it.
3. Select **Create account**.

You're signed in automatically and taken straight into the app.

Join links are single-use. Once you've set your password, the link no longer works. If a link has expired, ask your administrator to resend the invitation.

## How do I sign in?

1. Go to the login screen.
2. Enter your email address and password. To check your password, select the eye icon to reveal it.
3. Select **Sign in**.

Shybird Define remembers your email address for next time. It never remembers your password.

## What if I've forgotten my password?

1. On the login screen, select **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your email address and select **Send reset link**. For security, you see the same confirmation whether or not the email address is registered.
3. Open the link in the email. Set a new password of at least 8 characters, confirm it, then select **Update password**.

You're returned to the login screen. Resetting your password signs out your other sessions.

To change your password while you're signed in, see [Change your password]({% link _pages/define/notifications-and-settings.md %}#how-do-i-change-my-password).
