Each Shybird Define project has 19 modules, which you'll find on the module roadmap. You work through them in order to create your qualification, always moving forward in number order (0, 1, 2 and so on up to 18).

## What are the five phases?

The 19 modules are grouped into five phases.

| Phase | Modules | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1: Scoping]({% link _pages/define/phase-1-scoping.md %}) | 0 to 2 | Create your project, using your organisation's documents as input to define the project's parameters. |
| [2: Research]({% link _pages/define/phase-2-research.md %}) | 3 to 9 | Build the synthetic panel, then gather and synthesise government and international labour-market evidence. |
| [3: Design]({% link _pages/define/phase-3-design.md %}) | 10 to 15 | Design the unit structure of the course, then create indicative content and size the course. |
| [4: Validation]({% link _pages/define/phase-4-validation.md %}) | 16 | Benchmark the course design against comparable courses and funding eligibility. |
| [5: Publication]({% link _pages/define/phase-5-publication.md %}) | 17 and 18 | Generate and edit the final qualification specification, then format and publish it. |

## What happens in each module?

Here's what each module is called and what you do in it. The phase pages have a detailed walkthrough of every module.

| Module | Name | Your job in one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | [Project Creation]({% link _pages/define/phase-1-scoping.md %}#module-0-project-creation) | Upload strategy, business case and design rules documents, then review the extracted facts. |
| 1 | [Context Documents]({% link _pages/define/phase-1-scoping.md %}#module-1-context-documents) | Attach reference documents (regulator rules, an example specification, a verb bank) and choose shared knowledge-base documents. |
| 2 | [Perimeter Validation]({% link _pages/define/phase-1-scoping.md %}#module-2-perimeter-validation) | Confirm and edit the qualification's scope before research begins. |
| 3 | [Synthetic Panel Generation]({% link _pages/define/phase-2-research.md %}#module-3-synthetic-panel-generation) | Create the synthetic expert panel that reviews later steps. |
| 4 | [Government Data Landscape Analysis]({% link _pages/define/phase-2-research.md %}#module-4-government-data-landscape-analysis) | Pull and cluster UK government evidence. The panel reviews the themes. |
| 5 | [LMI (Labour Market Intelligence) Landscape Analysis]({% link _pages/define/phase-2-research.md %}#module-5-lmi-labour-market-intelligence-landscape-analysis) | Identify international lead markets and gather their labour-market evidence. |
| 6 | [Synthesis of Gov + LMI Themes]({% link _pages/define/phase-2-research.md %}#module-6-synthesis-of-gov--lmi-themes) | Merge the government and international themes into one set. |
| 7 | [KSB generation from job ads]({% link _pages/define/phase-2-research.md %}#module-7-ksb-generation-from-job-ads) | Fetch live job adverts, generate candidate KSBs and run a panel review. |
| 8 | [Data Analysis: Job Profiles]({% link _pages/define/phase-2-research.md %}#module-8-data-analysis-job-profiles) | Fetch occupational profiles and generate KSBs. The panel validates them. |
| 9 | [Synthesis of Job Ads and Job Profiles]({% link _pages/define/phase-2-research.md %}#module-9-synthesis-of-job-ads-and-job-profiles) | Merge the two KSB lists into one final list without duplicates. |
| 10 | [Unit Structure]({% link _pages/define/phase-3-design.md %}#module-10-unit-structure) | Decide the sorting pathway from your design rules. |
| 11 | [Sorting Functions]({% link _pages/define/phase-3-design.md %}#module-11-sorting-functions) | Assemble KSBs into a tree of units, learning outcomes and KSBs. |
| 12 | [Set & Confirm Content Levels]({% link _pages/define/phase-3-design.md %}#module-12-set--confirm-content-levels) | Assign an RQF level (1 to 8) to every KSB. |
| 13 | [Determine / Map Assessment Methods]({% link _pages/define/phase-3-design.md %}#module-13-determine--map-assessment-methods) | Map each unit to appropriate assessment methods. |
| 14 | [Indicative Content]({% link _pages/define/phase-3-design.md %}#module-14-indicative-content) | Generate teaching and learning content for each KSB. |
| 15 | [Size Course]({% link _pages/define/phase-3-design.md %}#module-15-size-course) | Estimate learning hours, and calculate GLH, TQT and credit. |
| 16 | [Benchmarking]({% link _pages/define/phase-4-validation.md %}#module-16-benchmarking) | Compare against existing qualifications for funding eligibility. |
| 17 | [Specification Generation]({% link _pages/define/phase-5-publication.md %}#module-17-specification-generation) | Assemble the full specification into sections you can edit. |
| 18 | [Final Publication]({% link _pages/define/phase-5-publication.md %}#module-18-final-publication) | Format the document, preview it, and publish and download it. |
