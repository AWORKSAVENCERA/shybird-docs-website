## What's in the sidebar?

When you sign in, you're taken to your dashboard, which shows all your current projects. The left sidebar is your main navigation. You can collapse or expand it, and Shybird Define remembers your choice.

The sidebar's main sections are:

- **Dashboard** - your projects home, where you can view all your current projects or start a new one.
- **Trash** - recently deleted projects, which you can restore or delete permanently. See [Delete and restore projects]({% link _pages/define/projects.md %}#how-do-i-delete-and-restore-a-project).
- **Notifications** (in the footer) - your notifications. A red badge shows how many are unread.
- **Settings** (in the footer) - manage your profile and security details.
- **Sign out** - leave the platform. Do this on shared devices.

When you're in a module, the sidebar changes to show the stages of the module roadmap.

## What's on the dashboard?

The dashboard greets you by name and gives an overview of your work:

- **Featured project** - your most recently updated project, with a progress ring, the current module, and a **Continue** button (or **View**, if the project is finished).
- **Stats cards** - 'In Flight' (projects in progress), 'Completed' (with a completion rate) and 'New this month'.
- **All Projects** - a searchable grid you can filter. Search by title, filter by 'All', 'Completed' or 'In progress', and select any card to resume that project where you left off.

Each project card shows:

- the title
- the occupation
- a 'GLH · TQT · RQF level' summary
- a completion percentage
- the current module, for example 'Module 7/18'
- when it was last edited.

Select the three dots in the top right corner of a project card to download the finished document (once the project is complete) or to delete the project.

## How do I get back to the dashboard?

From anywhere in a project, select the Avencera logo in the very top left of the page.
