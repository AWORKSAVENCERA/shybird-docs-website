## Module status

Unless a module is optional, you need to complete modules in order. Each module unlocks when you finish the one before it.

The button on the right of each module in your learning path shows its status:

| Button | What it means |
|---|---|
| Completed | You've finished this module. |
| Start Module | You can start this module now. |
| Complete prerequisite first | This module is locked until you finish the module before it. |
| Complete modules first | This module is locked until you finish all the modules. |

## Move through a module

Modules can contain videos, articles, links to other websites, quiz questions and rating-scale (Likert) questions.

- Complete the activities in each section, then select **Continue** in the bottom right to move on.
- Select **Exit Module** in the top right to go back to your learning path (the list of modules).

Your progress is saved automatically. You can leave a module at any time and carry on where you left off.

While you're in a module, you can ask the [Learning Assistant]({% link _pages/deliver/learning-assistant.md %}) to explain the content.

## Download your certificate

When you've completed everything in your learning path, the **Download Certificate** button becomes available. Select it to download your certificate of completion.

## When your course ends

You can still use Shybird Deliver for a limited time after your course ends. Before your access is removed, download and save your certificate and anything else you want to keep, such as materials, feedback or work you submitted. Check your organisation's policies first to make sure you're allowed to keep them.
