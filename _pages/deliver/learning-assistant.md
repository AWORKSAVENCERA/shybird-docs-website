The Learning Assistant is an AI-enabled chat tool. It answers your questions about your course and explains course content in a way that's personalised to you.

## Open the Learning Assistant

You can open the Learning Assistant in two ways:

- **From the menu.** Select **Content Questions** in the menu on the left, then type your question in the chat.
- **While you're in a module.** Select the multi-coloured Avencera logo in the bottom right of the screen to open the chat.

If the logo is in your way, drag it to another part of the screen.
