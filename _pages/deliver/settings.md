To open your settings, select **Settings** in the menu on the left. Then open **Profile and Security**, where you can manage your personal details, learning reminders and signed-in devices.

## Profile details

Your personal details are on the right of the panel. You can change your:

- full name
- email address
- department
- job title
- organisation name
- sector.

Make your changes, then select **Save Changes**.

If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, changing your email address here doesn't change the account you sign in with.

## Profile photo

Your profile photo is on the left of the panel. Select **Change Photo** to upload a new image, or **Remove Photo** to delete your current one.

## Learning reminders

Under **Learning Reminders**, choose how Shybird Deliver reminds you about courses you haven't finished:

- **Reminders** - get a nudge in Shybird Deliver when you haven't visited a course for a while. Use the switch to turn this on or off.
- **Email reminders** - get reminders by email as well. Use the switch to turn this on or off.
- **Pause reminders** - take a break without turning reminders off. Choose **Pause 2 weeks**, **Pause 1 month** or **Pause 3 months**.

## Active sessions

Under **Active Sessions**, you can see each device you're signed in on and when it was last active.

To sign out of a device, select **End Session** next to it. Do this if you've signed in on a shared or public computer.
