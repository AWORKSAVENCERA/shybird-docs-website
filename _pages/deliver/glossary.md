description: Definitions of the terms used in Shybird Deliver, including course, module, learning path, onboarding, Learning Assistant and Resource Library.
---

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Certificate | Your proof that you've completed a course. You can download it once you've finished everything in your learning path. |
| Course | A complete learning programme that you're enrolled on, made up of modules. |
| Enrolment | Being added to a course by your organisation's admin, which gives you access to it. |
| Learning Assistant | An AI-enabled chat tool that answers your questions and explains course content in a way that's personalised to you. |
| Learning path | The ordered list of everything in your course, showing what to complete and in what order. |
| Lesson | A single part of a module. |
| Module | A section of a course, made up of lessons. Each module is one step in your learning path. |
| Onboarding | A short set of questions you may be asked the first time you open a course. You only need to answer them once. |
| Resource Library | Resources and learning materials from all your courses, in one place. |
