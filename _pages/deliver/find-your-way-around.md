The menu on the left of the screen takes you to each part of Shybird Deliver.

| Menu item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Courses** | Your 'Course Overview' page. This is where you land when you sign in. It shows the courses you're enrolled on and other courses you can register interest in. See [Start your course]({% link _pages/deliver/start-your-course.md %}). |
| **Events** | The events you're signed up to. If you have none, you'll see 'No events available'. |
| **Resource Library** | Resources and learning materials from all your courses. If your courses have none, you'll see 'No resources available'. |
| **Content Questions** | The AI-enabled Learning Assistant, where you can ask questions about your course content. See [Use the Learning Assistant]({% link _pages/deliver/learning-assistant.md %}). |
| **Technical Questions** | Frequently asked questions and contact details for support. See [Get help]({% link _pages/deliver/get-help.md %}). |
| **Notifications** | Updates about your courses and activities. Use the filter in the top left to show all or only unread notifications. |
| **Settings** | Your profile, learning reminders and signed-in devices. See [Change your settings]({% link _pages/deliver/settings.md %}). |
| **Dark mode** | A switch to change between dark and light mode. |
