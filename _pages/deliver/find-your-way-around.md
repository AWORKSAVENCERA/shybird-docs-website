The menu on the left of the screen takes you to each part of Shybird Deliver.

| Menu item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Courses | Your 'Course Overview' page. This is where you land when you sign in. It shows the courses you're enrolled on and other courses you can register interest in. See [Start your course](/deliver/start-your-course). |
| Events | The events you're signed up to. If you have none, you'll see 'No events available'. |
| Resource Library | Resources and learning materials from all your courses. If your courses have none, you'll see 'No resources available'. |
| Content Questions | The AI-enabled Learning Assistant, where you can ask questions about your course content. See [Use the Learning Assistant](/deliver/learning-assistant). |
| Technical Questions | Frequently asked questions and contact details for support. See [Get help](/deliver/get-help). |
| Notifications | Updates about your courses and activities. Use the filter in the top left to show all or only unread notifications. |
| Settings | Your profile, learning reminders and signed-in devices. See [Change your settings](/deliver/settings). |
| Dark mode | A switch to change between dark and light mode. |
