## Find your courses

Your courses are on the 'Course Overview' page, which opens when you sign in. You can also get there by selecting **Courses** in the menu.

To show only the courses assigned to you, open the **All Courses** dropdown in the top right and select **Assigned**.

To open a course, select its **Start Course** button.

## Answer the onboarding questions

The first time you open a course, you may be asked some onboarding questions. These vary between courses. You only need to answer them once.

## Your learning path

After any onboarding questions, you'll see the course's learning path. It lists every part of your course in the order you should complete it, and shows your progress through each one. The course title and module reference are at the top of the page.

A learning path usually includes:

- **Pre-course questionnaire** - a short set of questions to answer before you begin.
- **Modules** - the main learning content. Each module is made up of lessons. It shows the estimated reading and watching time, the number of lessons, and a progress bar showing how many lessons you've completed.
- **Tests** - short assessments to check your understanding.
- **Post-course questionnaire** - a short set of questions to answer once you've finished all the modules.
- **Download certificate** - your certificate of completion.

What's included varies between courses. On some courses, some modules are optional.
