## Activate your account

Before you can sign in, your organisation's admin must enrol you on a course. When they do, you'll get an email from no-reply@avencera.ai.

1. Open the email and select **Activate Your Account**.
2. Follow the steps on the sign-up page.

You only need to activate your account once. After that, the activation link takes you to an 'Invitation Used' page.

## Sign in

1. Go to your organisation's Shybird Deliver sign-in page. Each organisation has its own web address, which you'll be given when you're enrolled. If you don't have it, ask your organisation's admin.
2. Enter the email address you were enrolled with, then select **Sign in**.
3. Check your email for a one-time verification code.
4. Enter the code to finish signing in.

### Sign in with Google or Microsoft

On the sign-in page, you can select **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft** instead. You'll be taken to Google or Microsoft to sign in, then returned to Shybird Deliver.

## Sign out

Select the sign-out icon to sign out of your account securely. You'll need to sign in again to use Shybird Deliver.

To sign out of another device, such as a shared or public computer, see [Active sessions]({% link _pages/deliver/settings.md %}#active-sessions).

## If you can't sign in

1. Check you're using the email address you were enrolled with.
2. Check your account has been activated. If the link in your activation email takes you to an 'Invitation Used' page, your account is already active - go to your organisation's sign-in page instead.
3. Check you're using your organisation's sign-in page.
4. Check your internet connection by opening a different website.
5. If you still can't sign in, contact your organisation's admin or helpdesk.
