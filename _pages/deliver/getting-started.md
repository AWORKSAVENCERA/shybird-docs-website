Shybird Deliver is the online platform for your course. Your course materials, learning activities, assessments and certificate are all in one place.

## Before you start

- **You need to be enrolled.** Your organisation's admin enrols you on a course. You'll then get an email inviting you to activate your account.
- **Use any modern web browser.** Shybird Deliver works in a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. The layout adjusts to fit smaller screens, so you can keep up with your course wherever you are.
- **It's always available.** You can use Shybird Deliver 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
